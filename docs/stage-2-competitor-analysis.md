The field splits by what triggers payment. Fiat booking rails take the capital: Passionfroot holds $21M and grew revenue 13x YoY (TechCrunch, 2026-07-22), Collabstr reached 900,000 registered users bootstrapped (Early Stage Journal, 2026-03-12). Crypto capital went to trading attention rather than escrowing it, with Noise raising $7.1M from Paradigm (The Block, 2026-01-14). The direct layer, the three sites selling the same four profile surfaces, has no disclosed funding, no disclosed GMV and no independently verified user count between them.

Pricing is where the stage 1 read needs updating. subheader.lol posts a $3/day minimum and a suggested $3-$5/day range (retrieved 2026-09-16), so a direct competitor has now published a per-day rate. At that level a 30-day banner clears $90-$150, against the $400 gross spot-month inferred from Headr.io's self-reported creator earnings and the $500 median booking OXAR needs for a 10% take to cover a 30-minute onboarding call. The risk is that posted price discovery settles an order of magnitude below the sizing input. OXAR's position today: pre-launch, zero deals closed, 3 people on the waitlist, unfunded.

### If a funded incumbent copies the release condition

Passionfroot is the one that could. It holds $21M and grew revenue 13x YoY (TechCrunch, 2026-07-22), and adding a scheduled read of a public profile is days of work at $0.00018 per lookup (twitterapi.io, 2026-09-16). Collabstr, at 900,000 registered users (Early Stage Journal, 2026-03-12), would have to break its own 48-hour brand-approval release (CreatorStackClub, 2026-06-11), so it is less likely. Agentio prices on measured outcomes, which profile surfaces cannot report.

What survives the copy is inventory, not code. First, the seller roster, the first 20-30 hand-onboarded accounts with posted per-spot prices, which a fiat incumbent cannot assemble quickly. Second, a published per-spot, per-week rate card, which no competitor has. Third, non-custodial USDC settlement, which a fiat platform will not adopt while its custody status under FinCEN stays Not found. None is a moat; all three are a head start measured in months.
